ABOUT US

Our founder, Margarita, is only 26, but she's already gone from an ordinary girl
to a sought-after model in Asia. It all began in 2024, when her Instagram photos
were accidentally spotted by scouts. An invitation to a modeling agency proved
life-changing: two years of shooting and working in various corners of Asia
gave her invaluable experience. It was there that Margarita was inspired to
create a space where aspiring models could feel confident and break into the
industry just as quickly.

Our agency's mission is to turn dreams into reality, relying on support
and professionalism. We don't just look for pretty faces: we develop personalities,
teach them how to be on camera, handle objections, and build a personal brand.
It's important to us that every newcomer feels confident, and that experienced
models find new horizons for growth.

We're building a community where individuality, honesty, and mutual support
are valued. We understand the demands of the international market and help
our models reach a global level. Today, we're a team that knows the path to
success, having recently walked it ourselves, from the first step to landing our
first contracts.

Ready to start your story? We're waiting for you.

Margarita, the founder of the Marge Model agency
